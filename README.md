# ClutchState Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 1 November 2026
**Publisher:** Chandran V V
**Contact:** chandranvenkat398@gmail.com

ClutchState is an Android app that helps you limit time in distracting apps, shows reminders from your own task list, and can pause YouTube Music after the current song. This policy explains what the app accesses and how it is handled.

## Summary

- ClutchState has no internet permission. It cannot send data off your device.
- It does not collect, share or sell personal information, and it has no accounts, ads or analytics.
- Everything it stores stays in the app's private storage on your device. Automatic cloud backup and device transfer of that data are turned off.

## Information stored on your device

ClutchState stores only what it needs to work:

- The apps you choose to limit (package name and display name), your daily allowance, reminder interval and reset time for each, and how much of the allowance has been used.
- The tasks you type into the app, and whether each is completed.
- Your settings, such as whether the YouTube Music feature is on and whether you accepted the accessibility disclosure, plus a small checkpoint used to keep usage counts correct after a restart.

This information is not transmitted anywhere. You can delete it at any time by removing items in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app.

## Permissions and how they are used

### Accessibility service

ClutchState uses an Android Accessibility Service only to learn which app is in the foreground, so it can measure time in the apps you chose to limit and show a blocking screen or a task reminder over them when you reach your limit. It reads only the package name of the app's window. It does not read screen text, what you type, passwords, messages or any other on-screen content, and it does not perform gestures or take actions on your behalf, apart from returning you to the home screen when you choose that option on the blocking screen. The service is not enabled until you accept an in-app disclosure and turn it on in Android Settings, and you can turn it off there at any time. Information from this service is used on the device and is never shared.

### Usage access

ClutchState uses Android's usage access permission to read app usage events on your device, so it can recover accurate usage totals if the device restarts or the service was interrupted. This data is processed locally and is not transmitted.

### Notification access (YouTube Music)

If you turn on the YouTube Music feature, ClutchState uses notification access to observe media-session information from YouTube Music only: track identity, title, artist, duration and playback position. It uses this to detect the end of the current song and pause playback. It ignores notifications from other apps, does not read notification content, and does not store or transmit track information.

### Widgets

The home-screen todo widgets display your saved tasks and let you tick them off. They read the same local data described above.

## Sharing

ClutchState does not share information with the publisher or any third party. It contains no advertising or analytics code.

## Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all of the app's data. There is no server-side copy to request deletion of.

## Children

ClutchState is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Security

Data is kept in Android's app-private storage. No method is perfectly secure, so protect your phone with a screen lock.
