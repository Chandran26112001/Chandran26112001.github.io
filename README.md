# BlackSlate Privacy Policy — draft

**Effective date:** 23/10/2026  
**Publisher:** Chandran V V  
**Contact:** chandranvenkat398@gmail.com

BlackSlate is a personal expense, shared-bill and lending record-keeping app. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use its features.

## Information stored by the app

BlackSlate stores your finance records on your device. These records can include transaction amounts, categories, notes, repayment history, names and phone numbers that you choose to add for shared expenses or lending, optional transaction photos, and optional location coordinates and place names.

The app does not create an account, connect to a bank, process payments or operate a BlackSlate cloud service. BlackSlate does not sell personal information.

## Optional device permissions

BlackSlate requests optional permissions only when you use the related feature:

- **Contacts:** to let you select a person for a shared expense or lending record. Contact data selected for a record is saved on your device as part of that record.
- **Location:** to save the location of a transaction and optionally turn it into a readable area name. Location saving is off until you enable it in the app.
- **Photos:** you choose images to attach to a transaction. Attached images are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

You can decline or later revoke permissions in Android Settings. The relevant optional feature will not work without its permission.

## Sharing and exports

When you choose to export a CSV file or share a settlement card, BlackSlate creates the selected content on your device and hands it to the app or destination you select. For example, a settlement card may be sent through WhatsApp if it is installed. The recipient app's privacy policy then applies.

## Location and maps

If location saving is enabled, BlackSlate records a current location with the transaction. When you ask to open a saved location in a map, BlackSlate sends a map intent to the map app or web destination on your device. The map provider's privacy policy applies to that interaction.

## Data retention and deletion

Your records remain on your device until you delete them in BlackSlate or remove the app and its app data. Exported files and content you share are managed by the destination you selected. To remove all locally stored BlackSlate records, delete transactions individually or clear BlackSlate's storage in Android Settings; uninstalling the app also removes its app-private files, subject to Android backup settings and any files you exported elsewhere.

## Security

BlackSlate keeps its working records in Android app-private storage. No security measure is absolute; protect your phone with a screen lock and use the optional app lock where available.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the publisher will update the effective date and make the updated policy available at the same public URL.
